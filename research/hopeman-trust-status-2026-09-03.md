# Hopeman Brothers asbestos-trust status review — September 3, 2026

## Question

Whether the Hopeman Brothers asbestos personal-injury trust is now incorporated, legally formed, operational, or accepting claims following plan confirmation.

## Primary-source findings

The official Verita Global docket identifies **Docket No. 1542**, filed August 20, 2026, as the *Order Confirming the Amended Plan of Reorganization of Hopeman Brothers, Inc. Under Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code and Approving Adequacy of the Disclosure Statement*. The order includes the Third Modified Amended Plan dated August 17, 2026. The plan provides for a “newly created Asbestos Trust” and channels specified asbestos-related liabilities to it under the proposed permanent channeling injunction.

Confirmation is not the same thing as public operational launch. The plan’s implementation provisions identify transactions on the **Effective Date** and list conditions precedent to that date. As of this review, the official case-agent landing page displays solicitation material and instructions for historical **non-asbestos** bankruptcy proofs of claim; it does not display a notice of plan effectiveness, a public asbestos-trust claim form, a trust-specific portal, a payment percentage, or filing instructions for asbestos trust claims.

The current official docket also records an active appeal: Docket No. 1549, filed September 1, 2026, is the Fourth Circuit’s notification that Case No. 26-2188 was opened after Century Indemnity Company and Westchester Fire Insurance Company filed a notice of appeal on August 28, 2026.

## Conclusion

Do **not** say the trust is “incorporated” or “open” based solely on confirmation. The available official record confirms a court-approved plan that provides for an asbestos trust, but does not establish for public-facing purposes that the plan has reached its Effective Date, that the trust is operating, or that it is accepting asbestos claims. “Incorporated” is also not necessarily the correct legal concept for a trust; the controlling question is whether the plan’s trust-formation and effective-date conditions have been satisfied.

## Article-safe wording

> On August 18, 2026, the court confirmed Hopeman Brothers’ Chapter 11 plan, which provides for a newly created asbestos personal-injury trust. As of September 3, 2026, the public case materials reviewed do not provide a notice that the plan is effective or public asbestos-trust claim-filing instructions; the confirmation order is also subject to a newly opened Fourth Circuit appeal. Readers should not assume that the trust is operational or accepting claims until the effective-date and claims-administration notices are published.
