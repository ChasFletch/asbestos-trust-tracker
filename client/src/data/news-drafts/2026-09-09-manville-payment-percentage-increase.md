# Manville Trust Raises Pro Rata Payment Percentage to 5.6%
date: 2026-09-09
category: payment_change
url: https://www.claimsres.com/2026/09/03/manville-increase-in-the-pro-rata-payment-percentage/

The Manville Trust’s administrator announced that the Trust’s **pro rata payment percentage increased from 5.1% to 5.6%**. The September 3, 2026 announcement says changes were implemented in e-Claims on September 2, including updated future and outstanding offers.[1]

The notice also describes planned mid-October retroactive supplemental-payment adjustments for claims the administrator identifies as eligible. It does not report a new trust balance, cumulative payout total, claimant-level eligibility decision, or individual payment amount.
