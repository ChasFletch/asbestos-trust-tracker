## The verified update

On October 12, 2026, a notice titled **“New SSN Policy”** was posted on behalf of nine asbestos trusts administered through the Delaware Claims Processing Facility (DCPF). The notice says that the trusts require a Social Security number (SSN) for a claim to be considered filed, including for the Trust Distribution Procedure (TDP) statute-of-limitations and first-in, first-out (FIFO) provisions.[1]

The notice applies to the following trusts:

1. Armstrong World Industries, Inc. Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust
2. Babcock & Wilcox Company Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust
3. Celotex Asbestos Settlement Trust
4. Federal-Mogul Asbestos Personal Injury Trust
5. Flintkote Asbestos Trust
6. Owens Corning/Fibreboard Asbestos Personal Injury Trust
7. The Pittsburgh Corning Corporation Personal Injury Settlement Trust
8. United States Gypsum Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust
9. WRG Asbestos PI Trust

Owens-Illinois / Paddock is **not** named in the notice. This draft does not extend the policy to any trust not on the published list.

## What the notice says

The notice gives firms a **30-day grace period from the date of the notice** to add SSNs to active or withdrawn claims that lack them. It says that after that grace period, claims without SSNs will be rejected and any refiled claim will receive statute-of-limitations and FIFO status as of its re-filing date.[1]

The notice also says that the policy applies to pending claims and to claims previously submitted without an SSN that are in Withdrawn or Deemed Withdrawn status. It states that, unless an SSN is supplied within the stated 30-day period, those claims are not deemed filed for statute-of-limitations purposes.[1]

The notice gives a relative period rather than a calendar cutoff. This draft therefore does **not** state a calculated deadline or make a claim-specific determination about any person’s filing status, queue position, limitations issue, or outcome.

## The linked DCPF instructions

The notice links to DCPF’s three-page **“Trust Online SSN Editing — October 2026”** instructions. The document identifies **Deficiency Code 003 — “Injured Party’s Social Security Number Not Provided”** and explains how Trust Online users can find claims with that deficiency and edit SSN information, including on Withdrawn and Deemed Withdrawn claims.[2]

The instructions describe the Trust Online workflow. They do not add a different trust list, announce a payment change, or determine whether a particular claim will be accepted.

## What remains unknown from these sources

The reviewed notice does not provide an individualized deadline, list every affected claim, establish whether any claim has been rejected, or announce a change in any trust’s payment percentage, assets, payouts, or claim value. It should not be read as individualized legal advice. Readers with claim-specific questions should use the trust’s listed claimant-relations channel or consult qualified counsel.
