# Celotex FY2025 Annual Report — No-Charge Recovery Pass

**Entity:** Celotex Asbestos Settlement Trust  
**Target:** FY2025 Annual Report, Summary of Claims Disposed, Financial Statement, and Account of the Trustee; Bankr. M.D. Fla. Case No. 8:90-bk-10016, Docket Doc. 14439, filed April 28, 2026.  
**Result:** **Not publicly retrieved; primary report remains unavailable to this pass.**

## Methods and Findings

The official Celotex Trust document page was reviewed directly. It makes current forms, procedure documents, and notices publicly available, but it does not list the FY2025 annual report or a downloadable annual-report archive. The official site continues to state the current 7% IRC percentage and to list its most recent public notices; those materials do not replace the requested annual report.[1]

The free CourtListener/RECAP search was also checked using the exact case number and docket-entry number. It returned no public result for the requested document. CourtListener notes that the RECAP archive does not contain every PACER document; this is an access limitation, not evidence that the filing does not exist.[2]

Public web discovery located a docket-text index confirming the FY2025 annual-report entry description, but not a filed PDF. That index is a discovery lead only and is not a controlling source for financial or claims figures.[3]

## Disposition

No public tracker figure, payment percentage, asset value, claim statistic, or status statement was changed. The existing FY2006 personal-injury-pool cumulative-payment figure remains a clearly dated historical record. The FY2025 report remains a **primary-source recovery gap** and should next be revisited through official trust-document pages, public court repositories, and public docket-mirror availability only. This task does not authorize a PACER purchase or paid retrieval.

## September 10 Corroboration Update

Two independent public docket indexes now corroborate the **existence and procedural sequence** of the FY2025 filing without supplying its substantive exhibits. Inforuptcy reproduces the April 28, 2026 docket text for Doc. 14439 and identifies the August 18, 2026 order at Doc. 14444 approving the annual-report application. CourtListener’s public docket likewise lists Doc. 14439 as a report filed April 28 and Doc. 14444 as an order-type entry filed August 18, but marks both main documents as available only through PACER.[4] [5]

This supports a limited conclusion: the FY2025 annual-report filing and a subsequent approval order occurred. It does **not** supply a controlling financial statement, claims table, payment total, or current trust figure. No public tracker fact changed. The official trust documents page and a 2026 Internet Archive CDX review did not produce either PDF.[1] [6]
