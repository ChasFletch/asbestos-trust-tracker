# Pittsburgh Corning Historical-Report Recovery — No-Charge Source Review

**Trust:** Pittsburgh Corning Corporation Asbestos PI Trust  
**Research date:** September 10, 2026  
**Status:** Docket metadata corroborated; filed report attachment not recovered  
**Public tracker effect:** None

## Research Question

The recovery task was to obtain a publicly accessible, controlling annual report or trustee account that could verify, correct, or replace the tracker’s qualified 2022 secondary cumulative-payment component. The work was limited to no-charge official, public court-index, archive, and community-request routes. No PACER purchase, paid record, or paid external service was used.

## What the Reviewed Sources Establish

The official PCC documents page provides trust forms, trust-distribution procedures, notices, and other operating documents, but it does not list annual reports or audited annual accounts. The official Trust Online Reports page describes claimant-facing reports available through the Trust Online system; it does not provide the filed annual-report series at issue.

The public CourtListener index for the Pittsburgh Corning bankruptcy docket corroborates the existence and descriptions of the relevant annual-report filings. It lists Doc. 10913 for the FY2018 report and account, Doc. 10920 for FY2019, Doc. 10921 for FY2020, Doc. 10940 for FY2021, Doc. 10942 for FY2022, and Doc. 10943 for FY2023. Each entry identifies annual-report attachments, but the publicly available index directs readers to unavailable or paid court-document paths rather than providing a free attachment copy.

| Source | Result | Evidentiary use |
|---|---|---|
| Official PCC documents page | No annual-report series listed | Confirms that the ordinary public trust document library did not yield the requested report |
| Official PCC Trust Online Reports page | Claimant-report portal described; no filed annual accounts posted | Does not establish annual-report figures |
| CourtListener docket, Case 00-22876 | Annual-report notice metadata confirmed for FY2018–FY2023 | Corroborates filing existence and scope, but not contents or figures |
| Public attachment paths | No free report attachment recovered | Does not support a filed-figure upgrade |

## Evidence Boundary and Next Path

The CourtListener entries are useful docket metadata, not the underlying annual reports. They do not independently establish cumulative payments or permit the qualified 2022 secondary component to be promoted to a filed figure. The tracker must retain its current qualification and historical labeling.

The next no-charge path is to monitor CourtListener/RECAP and the prior community-request status for a released attachment, revisit the official PCC document library after published updates, and inspect archive captures when the public site changes. A future report attachment must be reviewed for its exact period, table or page, scope, and payment definition before it affects tracker data.
