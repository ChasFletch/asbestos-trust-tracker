# W.R. Grace FY2025 Filing Evidence Note

**Access date:** September 22, 2026 (America/Chicago)  
**Court filing:** *In re W.R. Grace & Co., et al.*, U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, No. 01-01139-AMC, Doc. 33347, filed April 29, 2026.  
**Document title:** *Annual Report and Account of the WRG Asbestos PI Trust for the Fiscal Year Ending December 31, 2025.*  
**PACER charge accepted:** $3.40 for only two substantive attachments: Exhibit 1 (11 pages, $1.10) and Exhibit A to Exhibit 1 (23 pages, $2.30). The one-page cover document and two-page certificate of service were excluded.

## Verdict

**CONFIRMED, WITH SCOPE QUALIFICATION.** The filing directly supports the FY2025 net-assets figure of **$1,829,172,468**. It also supports an **approximate $2.69 billion** inception-to-date payment figure, but the reported amount is expressly limited to **Unliquidated PI Trust Claims**. The same narrative separately reports **approximately $30.22 million** for Pre-Petition Liquidated PI Trust Claims. The filing does **not** state one exact, combined all-category cumulative-payment total.

No tracker or aggregate figure was changed by this retrieval record.

## Derived-total boundary

The two separate approximations sum arithmetically to **approximately $2,720,220,000**. That is a **derived, category-combined approximation**, not a number stated by the filing. It should remain a research observation unless a subsequent reviewed release explicitly adopts it, explains that it combines the narrative’s two distinct claim categories, and preserves the filing’s approximation qualifiers. The existing $2.69 billion field is therefore a narrower **unliquidated-claims component**, not a verified all-category cumulative-payment total.
