## Unresolved evidence gaps for review

1. **Indian settlement aggregate:** Obtain, if available, a dated trustee-issued statement or ledger for the reported 3,849 settlements and £11.05 million. Needed fields: covered dates, whether payments are final/settled, currency basis, Indian-citizen definition, and whether totals include all Indian locations.
2. **Kymore-specific figures:** Obtain a trustee-published total for Kymore claims, payments, pending claims, and disease categories — or leave all Kymore-specific figures visibly secondary.
3. **Post-October 1, 2026 dividends:** Check trustee notices after the cited effective date for the new initial-dividend schedule and the outcome/timing of the autumn 2026 actuarial review. Do not infer it from FY2025 rates.
4. **Records transfer:** Obtain the underlying sale agreement, transfer schedule, or a written trustee statement that can substantiate the reported January 1, 1988 date and records-transfer description. Until then, retain the distinction between the primary year-level sale reference and the secondary exact-date statement.
5. **Eligibility scope:** Do not extrapolate the general public claim statement into a rule for a particular country, facility, person, disease diagnosis, or exposure category without the governing source and relevant fact record.
