# J.T. Thorpe Trust Announces 53.7% Payment Percentage; Implementation Is Still Pending
date: 2026-09-23
category: payment_change
url: https://www.jttstrust.com

The J.T. Thorpe Settlement Trust posted a **September 18, 2026** notice stating that its Trustees increased the Payment Percentage from **50% to 53.7% of total liquidated claim value**.[1] The Trust says claims will be paid under the change after necessary claims-processing-system and payment-procedure updates are completed. The notice does not state that implementation was complete on September 18 or provide a completed implementation date.

The tracker now records 53.7% as an **announced rate with implementation pending**. The notice does not report a new trust balance, cumulative payout, or claimant-specific payment. Read the **full source-linked brief** for the implementation boundary, the Trust’s statement about eligible previously paid claims, and the direct official source.
