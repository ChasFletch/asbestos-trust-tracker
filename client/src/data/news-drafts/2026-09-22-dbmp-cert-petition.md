# Asbestos Claimants Petition Supreme Court in DBMP Texas Two-Step Case

Asbestos claimants filed a petition for certiorari with the U.S. Supreme Court in *Herlihy v. DBMP, LLC* (No. 26-394), docketed September 22, 2026. The petition asks whether pursuing bankruptcy through a Texas Two-Step restructuring lacks good faith and provides cause to lift the automatic stay under § 362(d) of the Bankruptcy Code.

The petition follows the Fourth Circuit's February 11, 2026 decision affirming denial of stay relief — a split panel ruling that created a direct conflict with the Third Circuit's *Johnson & Johnson* decision on whether financial distress is required for Chapter 11. The DBMP case affects an estimated 60,000+ pending asbestos claims against the CertainTeed affiliate.
