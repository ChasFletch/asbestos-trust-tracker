| A177076 | 25CV161701 | *Minerals Technologies Inc. v. The Superior Court of Alameda County* | 5 | July 21, 2026 | August 7, 2026 | A separate Alameda Superior Court matter was the trial-court proceeding tied to the Godina Court of Appeal case; the case portal links it to S298147. |

## Corporate and bankruptcy context — source-qualified only

Two company-issued pages provide limited corporate context:

1. Minerals Technologies’ [June 29, 2026 statement](https://www.mineralstech.com/investors/news/news-details/2026/06/29/minerals-technologies-inc--files-plan-of-reorganization-in-bmi-oldco-chapter-11-cases-to-comply-with-court-deadline-30471) says that BMI OldCo Inc. is formerly Barretts Minerals Inc., that the debtors’ Chapter 11 cases are pending in the Southern District of Texas, and that a district-court threshold issue remains pending. The statement is a company position and is **not** a substitute for a court order, docket, or neutral legal characterization.
2. Minerals Technologies’ [Specialty Minerals page](https://www.mineralstech.com/specialty-minerals) describes Specialty Minerals Inc. as “a Minerals Technologies company.” This supports a corporate-relationship description only; it does not establish litigation responsibility, case merits, or an effect on the BMI OldCo cases.

A separate court document concerning *LaSalle v. American International Industries*, W.D. Wash. No. 2:26-cv-03159-TL, is preserved in `intake-2026-10-15/04-lasalle-remand.md`. It is a distinct remand order and is not evidence of the issues, holdings, or effect of the four California appellate matters.
