# Bestwall Stay-Relief Fight Heads to Fourth Circuit

The executor of a mesothelioma victim's estate filed an opening brief in the Fourth Circuit on August 18, 2026, arguing the appeals court should reverse a North Carolina bankruptcy court's denial of relief from the automatic stay in Bestwall LLC's Chapter 11 case. The brief contends the Georgia-Pacific spinoff's "Texas Two-Step" bankruptcy was filed in bad faith. Bestwall remains in Chapter 11, and Georgia-Pacific itself has not refiled since abandoning the Bestwall strategy. The parallel Supreme Court petition in the DBMP/CertainTeed case (Herlihy v. DBMP, No. 26-394) raises the same good-faith question.
