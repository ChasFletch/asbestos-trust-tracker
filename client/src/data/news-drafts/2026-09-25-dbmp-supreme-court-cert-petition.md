# DBMP Claimants Ask Supreme Court to Review Texas Two-Step Bankruptcy

date: 2026-09-25
category: court_filing
url: https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-394/424752/20260917233151794_Herlihy%20Petition%20for%20Certiorari.pdf

A **September 17, 2026 petition for certiorari** asks the U.S. Supreme Court to review a Fourth Circuit decision involving DBMP, LLC and the use of a Texas divisional merger in an asbestos bankruptcy.[1] The Supreme Court docket lists the petition in **No. 26-394** and a response deadline of October 22, 2026. The filing is a request for review—not a grant, merits decision, payment change, or new trust term.

Read the **full source-linked brief** for the question presented, the petition’s stated connection to a proposed §524(g) trust, and what the public docket does not establish.
