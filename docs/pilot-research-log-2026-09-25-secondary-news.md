## Review and publication gate

Both staged articles concern bankruptcy procedure and statutory trust structure. Under the project’s editorial standards, their legal-context language requires **actual qualified legal review** before publication. No Paul Danziger or Rod de Llano article-specific review has occurred, so neither name is credited as a reviewer.

Before any public release, the applicable draft must receive a dated reviewer name and scope, then complete content integration, targeted and full regression tests, TypeScript and production builds, SSR/crawler and sitemap verification, and desktop/mobile review. Any release record must carry that review evidence; otherwise it remains staged.
