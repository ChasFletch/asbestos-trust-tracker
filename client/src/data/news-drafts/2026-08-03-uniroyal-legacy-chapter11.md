# Uniroyal Legacy Unit Files Chapter 11 Asbestos Bankruptcy

A legacy unit of defunct tire maker **Uniroyal** filed for Chapter 11 protection in New Jersey on July 31, 2026, seeking to resolve approximately **35,000 asbestos injury claims** through a prepackaged plan of reorganization. The filing marks the latest major legacy-industry entity to enter asbestos bankruptcy and could result in a new Section 524(g) trust.

The debtor also seeks to hand over administration of ongoing retiree benefit programs. A trust has not yet been established; the case is in early-stage proceedings.
