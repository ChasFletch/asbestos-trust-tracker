## Primary-Source Boundary

The publicly described CourtListener filings establish that later annual-report and financial-statement attachments were filed in the USG bankruptcy case. They do not disclose those attachments’ content, and the reviewed pages say the attachments are not in the free RECAP collection. The official USG website’s current notices remain valid payment-rate evidence, but they cannot establish cumulative payouts. Consequently, the tracker continues to label the 2008 amount as a **historical floor**.

## Public Recovery-Card Update

The Source Recovery card now identifies the official-library and public-index limits, names the later docket entries, and explicitly distinguishes filing metadata from unretrieved attachments. It neither updates a trust figure nor converts a current payment notice into historical-payout evidence.
