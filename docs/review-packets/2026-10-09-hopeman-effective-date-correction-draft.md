## Proposed article copy

The official Hopeman Brothers case record now establishes two milestones that were not available when this article was first published: the confirmed plan became effective on **September 15, 2026**, and the plan documents provide that the Asbestos Trust was established on that Effective Date. The record does **not** provide a current payment percentage, a current net-asset balance, a public claim form or portal, or claimant-specific filing instructions.[1] [2]

## What the public case record confirms

The **U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia** issued the confirmation order on **August 18, 2026**. The order was then filed on the Hopeman Brothers bankruptcy docket as **Docket No. 1542** on **August 20, 2026**. The order confirms the amended plan and approves the related disclosure statement.[1]

The official Notice of Entry and Occurrence of Effective Date, **Docket No. 1551**, states that the Plan’s Effective Date occurred on **September 15, 2026** and that the plan was substantially consummated. It also states that the plan’s §524(g) asbestos permanent channeling injunction became effective on that date.[2]

The plan provisions incorporated in the confirmation record provide that, on the Effective Date, the Asbestos Trust was established, the Asbestos Trust Contribution was made, and the Asbestos Trust Assets vested in the trust. The same provisions describe the trust as created under the plan documents and §524(g) to process, liquidate, and pay compensable channeled asbestos claims under those governing documents.[1]

> **Confirmed by the reviewed record:** The District Court confirmed the plan; the plan became effective September 15, 2026; and the plan documents provide that the Asbestos Trust was established, funded through the stated contribution, and vested with its trust assets on that date.
>
> **Not established by the reviewed record:** A current payment percentage, a current trust-wide asset balance, cumulative payments, a public claims portal, a public claim form, or claimant-specific filing instructions.

## Why an effective plan does not answer every claimant-facing question

An effective plan and an established trust do not, by themselves, publish the operating details needed to describe a public claims process. The reviewed documents do not supply a current payment percentage, a current financial balance, a Trust Distribution Procedure, a public filing form, a public portal, or individualized guidance about whether, when, or how any person may file a claim.[1] [2]

For that reason, AsbestosTrusts.org does not include Hopeman in its active-trust tracker, payment-percentage records, net-asset totals, or cumulative-payment totals. The article does not infer a current payment amount or trust balance from the plan language.

## The appeal record remains distinct

The official case docket also records a Fourth Circuit appeal notification. **Docket No. 1549**, entered September 1, 2026, states that Case No. 26-2188 was opened after Century Indemnity Company and Westchester Fire Insurance Company filed a notice of appeal on August 28, 2026.[3]

The appeal notification does not negate the Effective Date notice or independently determine the plan’s ultimate legal outcome. It is a separate docket event and should not be used to infer a current payment percentage, an open claims process, or an individual claimant outcome.[2] [3]
