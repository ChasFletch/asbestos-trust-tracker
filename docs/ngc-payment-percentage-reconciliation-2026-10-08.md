## Official-source evidence

### 1. Dated Trustee implementation notice — 45% effective January 19, 2023

- **Official source:** [NGCBIT PSP Increase to 45% & ALV Inflation Adjustment (PDF)](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/assets/documents/Notices/NGCBIT_Pmt_Percentage_Increase_45.pdf)
- **Document date / locator:** January 19, 2023; pages 1–2.
- **Field:** NGC Bodily Injury Trust Payment Sum Percentage (PSP).
- **Prior / new value:** 40% → **45%**.
- **Effective date:** **January 19, 2023**.
- **Consent / action:** The notice says that, with the consent of the Trust Advisory Committee (TAC) and Legal Representative (LR), the Trustees voted unanimously to increase the PSP to 45% and make supplemental payments to claimants previously paid at less than 45%.
- **Relevant text (page 1):**

> “On behalf of the Trustees of the NGC Bodily Injury Trust (the ‘Trust’), the Trust is pleased to inform you of the increase in the Trust’s Payment Sum Percentage (‘PSP’) from 40% to 45% … With the consent of the Trust Advisory Committee (‘TAC’) and the Legal Representative (‘LR’), the Trustees voted unanimously to increase the PSP to 45% … The PSP increase became effective January 19, 2023.”

- **Implementation details (page 1):**
  - Claims allowed before January 19, 2023 at 40% but not yet paid were to have updated NODAs and be paid at **45%** after return of the release.
  - Claims already allowed at 40% and in the payment queue were to be paid at **45%**.
  - Claims previously paid at 40% were to receive a supplemental payment based on 45%, net of prior payments.
  - Claims allowed on or after January 19, 2023 were to use the increased **45%** PSP, with the stated ALV-inflation-adjustment conditions.

**Interpretation:** This is a specific, dated, consent-backed Trustee action with an effective date and implementation instructions. It supports the tracker’s 45% value but cannot, alone, rule out a later change.

### 2. Later official implementation update — payment processing at 45%

- **Official source:** [NGCBIT Payment Percentage Increase Update (DOCX)](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/assets/documents/Notices/NGCBIT%20Payment%20Percentage%20Increase%20Update.docx)
- **Official listing context:** [NGC Notices page](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/notices.html).
- **Posted date:** **February 16, 2023** (shown by the official document rendering).
- **Field:** Implementation of the 45% PSP for claims then allowed but not yet paid.
- **Relevant text:**

> “The Notice of Determination (‘NODA’) letters for claims currently allowed but not yet paid have been updated to reflect the increased payment percentage of 45%.”
>
> “Once the properly executed release is returned and any outstanding deficiencies are cured, the claim can be processed for payment at 45%.”

**Interpretation:** This later official update corroborates the January 19 notice’s implementation, not merely an announced future rate. It does not announce a reversal or a different rate.

### 3. Current official FAQ — contradictory undated 40% statement

- **Official source:** [NGC FAQ](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/faq.html) (the linked [official FAQ DOCX](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/assets/documents/Filing%20Instructions/Frequently%20Asked%20Questions.docx) presents the same FAQ content).
- **Field:** “What is the NGCBIT Payment Percentage?”
- **Visible date / scope qualification:** **None established** for this FAQ answer. The page includes dates for some unrelated policy answers, but no date or sub-account/claim-class qualification accompanies this payment-percentage answer.
- **Relevant text:**

> “The NGCBIT Payment Percentage is currently 40%.”

**Interpretation:** This is an official, direct contradiction. It cannot be discarded as a historical page, clerical carryover, or stale statement without source-supported evidence. It also does not state that it supersedes the January/February 2023 documents, provide a later effective date, identify a Trustee action, or distinguish a claim cohort.

### 4. Claims Resolution Procedures — governing mechanism, no numeric current rate

- **Official source:** [Eighth Amended Claims Resolution Procedures (CRP) (PDF)](https://www.ngcbitrust.org/assets/documents/Resources/CRP.pdf)
- **Locator:** §2.2, “Determination of Payment Amounts,” PDF page 6 (document page 6).
- **Relevant text:**

> “The Trustees, in their discretion after consultation with the NGC Bodily Injury Trust Advisory Committee and Legal Representative, may establish the NGC Bodily Injury Trust Payment Percentage in effect from time to time.”
>
> “The Trustees shall determine the NGC Bodily Injury Trust Payment Percentage prior to making any distribution, and the Trustees shall reconsider the determination … at least every two years …”

**Interpretation:** The CRP explains who may set and reconsider the percentage and permits supplemental payments when a current percentage is higher than a prior paid percentage. It does **not** itself set a current numerical percentage, so it cannot resolve the 45%/40% conflict.
