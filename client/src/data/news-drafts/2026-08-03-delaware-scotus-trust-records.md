# Delaware Supreme Court Ends J&J Bid To Preserve Asbestos Trust Claims Records

The **Delaware Supreme Court** ruled on July 31, 2026, that major asbestos defendants including **Johnson & Johnson** and **Dow Chemical** cannot use an equitable bill of discovery to stop bankruptcy trusts from deleting decades of asbestos claims records. The decision reverses a prior Chancery Court ruling and affirms trusts' autonomy over data-retention policies.

The ruling has broad implications for trust governance and record-keeping practices across the asbestos bankruptcy trust system.

**Source:** Law360, "Del. Supreme Court Ends J&J Bid To Preserve Asbestos Data," July 31, 2026.
