## Narrow holding

The Court **granted Plaintiff’s motion to remand** and **remanded the case to King County Superior Court**. It also denied as moot PTI Union’s request for clarification about whether Union was currently a defendant. The ruling is limited to the motion to remand and the removal-timing/jurisdiction discussion; this note makes no finding about liability, trust funding, creation of any trust, or claim outcomes. Locators: PDF p. 1 (grant/remand statement); PDF p. 12, § IV “CONCLUSION” (disposition and PTI Union request).

The order’s reasoning applies the Ninth Circuit’s “unequivocally clear and certain” removal-timing standard and concludes that it was unequivocally clear and certain that the case was removable before A-I-I removed it. Locator: PDF pp. 5–11, especially p. 11 (final timing conclusion).
