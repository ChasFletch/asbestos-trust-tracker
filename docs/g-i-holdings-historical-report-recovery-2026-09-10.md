## Primary-Source Boundary

The official public site establishes current trust notices and procedures, while the public bankruptcy index confirms that historical special-purpose financial-statement packages were filed. Neither source makes the underlying report supporting the tracker’s **qualified 2022 component** available without paid retrieval. The 2022 payment-percentage notice cannot establish cumulative payments.

The tracker therefore retains the component’s current qualified label and does not promote it to a filed figure, alter its amount, or present it as a current trust-wide payout total.
