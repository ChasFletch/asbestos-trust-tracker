# Post-Pilot Payment-Rate and Annual-Report Monitoring Authorization

**Authorization date:** October 6, 2026  
**Authorization window:** October 6–November 5, 2026, inclusive  
**Time zone:** America/Chicago  
**Status:** Active only for the limited workflow stated below; it is not a renewal of the September 6–October 5 living-tracker pilot.

## Purpose and scope

This authorization resumes **routine monitoring for asbestos-trust payment-percentage changes and annual-report postings only**. It applies to the existing reviewed registry of official trust, administrator, case-agent, court, government, and primary-document sources. It does not authorize research or public changes outside those two monitoring classes.

The workflow may detect and verify:

- a newly posted payment-percentage notice or current official rate statement;
- the exact prior and newly stated rate, scope (including sub-account limits), source publication date, and effective, interim, proposed, or implementation status; and
- a newly posted or revised annual report, trustee account, financial statement, or claims report, including the trust or sub-account, reporting period, filing or publication date, and document URL or identifier; and
- an access failure or source replacement need that directly blocks rate verification.

The workflow may not infer a rate, financial figure, or claims statistic from a stale source, a missing document, an unchanged fingerprint, a search-result snippet, a secondary legal-marketing website, or a reader transport. A transport may identify a page for review, but the controlling official material must support any candidate.

## Cadence and resource limits

- **Weekdays at 1:00 PM Central:** review the existing source registry, candidate queue, recent run records, and coverage findings before conducting a payment-rate and annual-report pass.
- **Daily cap:** at most 25 registered sources and 12 minutes for initial detection across both monitoring classes.
- **Candidate review:** at most three material rate or annual-report candidates and 25 minutes total. A single candidate may receive one extension to 60 minutes only when a controlling official source has been identified and needs reconciliation.
- **No-charge only:** no PACER purchases, paid records, paid APIs, or paid external tools.
- **No expansion by default:** new source URLs require a recorded source-registry decision before they are used for a public fact.

## Approval gates

1. **Detect and deduplicate.** Treat any fingerprint or access result as an internal signal, not a fact. Deduplicate by trust, source, and newest dated controlling event.
2. **Verify the controlling source.** For a rate candidate, retain the source URL or identifier, document page/table where applicable, publication date, relevant effective or interim date, rate scope, and any consent or implementation qualification. For an annual-report candidate, retain the trust or sub-account, reporting period, source date, document page/table for any material field, unit, and as-of or effective date. Never treat a report’s annual cash payments, scheduled values, or one sub-account as a current trust-wide balance or rate.
3. **Record internally.** Add the evidence, old/new values, scope, and remaining uncertainty to the existing project queue. Access-only findings stay operational records.
4. **Prepare, do not publish.** A verified rate or annual-report candidate may be assembled as a proposed tracker correction or a review packet, but the workflow must not edit public data, create a public article, publish source code, or deploy. A document labelled “revised” is reported as revised without inferring why.
5. **Obtain a specific release decision.** A public tracker correction requires an explicit follow-up authorization after the evidence packet is complete. A substantive article, court-development explanation, or interpretive item must use the ANCHOR → CLEO → Charles pipeline. Paul Danziger or Rod De Llano may be credited only when actual article-specific reviewer, scope, and date are recorded.

## Boundaries retained

- Keep all routine findings in the existing project queue; do not send email or Slack.
- Do not touch ads, other websites, individualized advice, new firm positions, or unrelated trust facts.
- Do not represent a proposed, interim, consent-pending, or sub-account rate as an unqualified trust-wide implemented rate.
- Do not present historical funding, annual claim payments, reported assets, scheduled claim values, or a bounded partial series as a current total.
- Missing public information remains **not publicly reported** or **unknown**, never zero.

## End condition

The schedule expires at the start of November 6, 2026, Central time. It must not be extended automatically. At expiry, stop routine checks and prepare one bounded authorization assessment summarizing rate checks, candidates, access blockers, prepared correction packets, and whether another explicit decision is warranted.
