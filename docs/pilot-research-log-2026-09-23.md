## J.T. Thorpe release boundary

The J.T. Thorpe notice supports a narrow, material update. The public tracker records the new **53.7%** amount, the notice date, and the direct official source URL. It does **not** infer an effective date, claim that the administrative updates are complete, calculate individual claims, or change the trust’s December 31, 2025 net-asset figure or separately sourced cumulative-payment figure.

The notice also says that eligible previously paid claims **should** receive additional amounts due by the end of November 2026. This is retained as a prospective statement by the Trust rather than treated as proof that supplemental payments have been issued or that any particular person will receive payment.[1]
