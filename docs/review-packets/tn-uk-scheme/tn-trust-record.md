## Scope and classification

This record covers the UK scheme administered by **The T&N Asbestos Trustee Company Limited**, including the T&N UK Asbestos Trust and the T&N EL Trust. The trustee describes those trusts as a mechanism to compensate people exposed to asbestos by companies in the UK Federal-Mogul Group (formerly the T&N Group), under Trust Distribution Procedures (TDPs). [1]

This is the site’s first UK scheme record. It is **not** a U.S. § 524(g) trust, and this page does not apply U.S. trust terminology or U.S. court/docket fields to the scheme. Where the trustee’s materials use terms such as *Trust Claim*, *Established Claim*, *TDP*, *Fund*, and *dividend*, this record uses those terms. It does not determine whether any person has an eligible claim, what any individual claim is worth, or whether a particular exposure meets the scheme’s criteria.

## Trustee, scheme documents, and reporting structure

The trustee’s document library publishes the UK and EL TDPs, trustee annual reports through FY2025, and separate FY2025 financial statements for the UK Asbestos Trust and the T&N EL Scheme Trust. [2]

The FY2025 trustee report describes the T&N UK Asbestos Trust as four separate funds — the T&N Fund, Chester Street Fund, TBA Fund, and FMFP Fund — whose cash and investments must remain separate under the Trust Deed. (p. 1) [3] The report also identifies the T&N Asbestos Trustee Company Limited as the trustee. (p. 1) [3]

## Reported assets and payments

The UK Asbestos Trust’s audited FY2025 accounts report **£17,143,094** in net assets at September 30, 2025, comprising the separately reported Chester Street Fund and T&N Fund reserves in that account. The accounts say they do not account for compensation-claim obligations falling due after the end of the trust year. (pp. 4, 10–11) [4]

The T&N EL Scheme Trust’s FY2025 audited accounts report **£4,809,098** in net assets at September 30, 2025, and carry the same limitation concerning compensation-claim obligations after year-end. (pp. 3–4, 10–11) [5]

For FY2025, the trustee reported **526** Trust Claims received and **524** settled for payment, with **£4.78 million** paid to Trust Claimants. The report’s payment table separates the annual total among the T&N Fund, T&N EL Trust, Chester Street, FMFP, TBA, and Cape Trust Claims. (p. 2) [3]

### Cape category reported by the trustee

The trustee reported **358 Cape Trust Claims received**, **357 settled for payment**, and **£0.78 million** paid for the Cape category in FY2025. It says it continues to receive Cape Trust Claims from India in batches, but the table does not publish a Kymore subtotal or establish that every Cape-category figure is from Kymore. (pp. 1–3) [3]

The FY2025 report states that Cape Trust Claims are received from two former T&N subsidiaries, Hindustan Ferodo and Asbestos Cement. It identifies Kymore as one of the former Asbestos Cement factory locations. (p. 3) [3]

## Claim and payment mechanics documented by the trustee

The trustee’s public claims page says a claim may be made by a person who was exposed to asbestos dust during employment with a T&N company, or through the activities of a T&N company, **and** developed an asbestos-related disease. The trustee’s homepage says both employee and non-employee claims may be submitted. [1] [8]

The trustee directs applicants to submit a completed Proof of Claim form and supporting documents. Its application materials describe two routes, **Expedited Review** and **Individual Review**. The visual process documents show that a claim may be admitted as an *Established Claim* or rejected, and that an admission or rejection may be referred to the scheme’s Expert; they show a first payment following an accepted admission and consideration of an additional dividend later. [9] [10] [11]

The UK TDP says it sets the procedures for claims seeking payment from the Trust Funds. It provides for expedited and individual review, applies the current T&N Payment Percentage to an established claim in the payment provisions, and provides for periodic review of payment percentages and additional-dividend levels. (pp. 7, 14–15, 23, 41, 61) [12]

These are **scheme-level descriptions only**. The sources reviewed do not establish a separate published eligibility rule or payment formula specifically for Kymore claimants, Indian citizens, or any individual. The original trustee documents should control if this record is reviewed for publication.

## Kymore-related records gap

Kymore appears here only as context for the scheme’s claim record, not as a facility-history or contamination record.

The FY2025 trustee report says that Asbestos Cement had factories in Mumbai, Kolkata, Kymore, and Coimbatore, and that the business was sold to Eternit Everest at the beginning of 1988. (p. 3) [3] In a September 25, 2026 report, the Times of India quoted Anne O’Keefe, a director of the T&N Asbestos Trustee Company Ltd, as saying that the Trust had no records relating to the Kymore plant. The report said the factory was sold to Eternit Building Products Ltd on January 1, 1988, and that company records were understood to have transferred with the sale. [6]

This is a **documented records-availability gap**. It is not an accusation, a finding about responsibility, a conclusion about exposure, or a statement that records do not exist elsewhere. The underlying sale documentation and any records-transfer evidence were not obtained for this draft.
