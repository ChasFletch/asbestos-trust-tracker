# Miyoshi Chapter 11 Case Closes; Confirmed Talc Trust Structure Remains Governed by the Plan

date: 2026-09-25
category: court_filing
url: https://cases.stretto.com/public/x513/14526/PLEADINGS/1452609212680000000469.pdf

The U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas entered a **Final Decree on September 21, 2026** closing Miyoshi America, Inc.’s Chapter 11 case.[1] The decree says it does not change the amount or nature of any distribution or other substantive right under the confirmed plan, and it authorizes distributions consistent with that plan.[1]

This is a **talc personal-injury trust** case, not a newly added asbestos tracker trust. Read the **full source-linked explainer** for the distinction between case closure, a confirmed and effective §524(g) plan, and public facts that the decree does not supply.
