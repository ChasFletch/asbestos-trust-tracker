## Research Interpretation

The research pass found a plausible public, no-charge lead for every one of the **24 unique** source-gap trusts. The pilot database previously showed 44 blocked candidate rows because an early identifier-repair transition wrote a second audit row for each initial gap; this is an internal duplication issue, not evidence of 44 distinct uncovered trusts. The sources above should therefore be admitted only after a direct registration review and used with a fallback record rather than assumed to be current notice feeds.

The first scheduled daily detection recorded nine access failures among 25 attempted sources. Those failures are managed separately from the source-gap leads: they must be classified by HTTP/access behavior and resolved through source-specific, no-charge fallbacks, without accepting an unverified mirror or treating an inaccessible source as a no-change result.
