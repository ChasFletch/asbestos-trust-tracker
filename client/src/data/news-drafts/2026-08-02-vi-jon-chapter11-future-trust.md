# Vi-Jon Files Chapter 11 With $25M Asbestos-Talc Trust Plan (Aug 2, 2026)

Vi-Jon LLC, a private-label personal care and bath products manufacturer facing a reported 367 mesothelioma and lung cancer claims tied to asbestos-contaminated talc, filed a prepackaged Chapter 11 case in the District of Delaware on August 2, 2026. The proposed plan would channel talc injury claims into a trust funded by $25 million in cash. On September 14, 2026, the debtor asked the bankruptcy court to approve the plan's disclosure statement on an interim basis. The trust is not yet established and is not yet tracked in the figures dataset.
