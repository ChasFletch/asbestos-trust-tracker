# Vi-Jon hearing-date verification — private intake note

**Checked:** 15 October 2026  
**Case:** *In re Vi-Jon, LLC*, Chapter 11 Case No. 26-11216 (MFW), District of Delaware  
**Scope:** Official Omni case-agent documents only; no paid retrieval attempted.

## Source-proven finding

Omni’s free official copy of **Docket 290**, filed **October 13, 2026**, is an **Amended Notice of Agenda of Matters Scheduled for Hearing on October 14, 2026 at 11:30 a.m. (ET)**. On **PDF page 1 of 8**, it states in bold that, because there are no matters scheduled to go forward, **“THIS HEARING HAS BEEN CANCELLED WITH PEMRISSION OF THE COURT”** (the document’s apparent typographical error is reproduced as written). The October 14 agenda therefore was canceled; this is not evidence that the hearing occurred.

The disclosure-statement matter is separately listed under “Matters Going Forward.” On **PDF page 5 of 8**, Docket 290 identifies the matter as the Debtor’s disclosure-statement motion (Docket 143), lists **Docket 260** as the notice of the continued hearing originally scheduled for **October 14, 2026 at 11:30 a.m. (ET)**, and states: **“This matter has been adjourned by agreement of the parties to October 21, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. (ET).”** Thus, Docket 290’s operative disclosure-statement entry is an adjournment from the October 14 setting to October 21 at 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time, while the October 14 agenda itself is marked canceled because no matters remained scheduled to go forward.

The underlying free official **Docket 260** notice (PDF pages 1–2) confirms the prior setting: “NOTICE OF CONTINUED HEARING ON DISCLOSURE STATEMENT MOTION SCHEDULED FOR OCTOBER 14, 2026 AT 11:30 A.M. (ET),” and says the Court indicated at the October 1 hearing that it would continue the hearing to that date. The free official **Docket 277** agenda (PDF page 2, item 2; filed October 9) likewise listed the disclosure-statement motion as going forward on October 14 at 11:30 a.m. (ET), before Docket 290 amended the agenda.

No conclusion is made here that the October 21 setting occurred or remained unchanged. No conclusion is made that any trust was confirmed.

## Retained uncertainty

The reviewed official documents establish the October 13 docketed notice and its stated adjournment, but this bounded check does not establish what happened after Docket 290, including whether the October 21 setting was later changed, held, or decided. Docket 260 was recoverable free from Omni; no paid retrieval was attempted. Omni’s case website itself cautions that it is not the Bankruptcy Court’s website and is not a substitute for the complete official court record.
