## Bounded verified findings

1. **Secondary exposure — TDP Section 5.5.** The two-page resolution states it is effective **November 14, 2025**. For a claimant alleging disease from exposure to an occupationally exposed person, it generally requires Individual Review under TDP Section 5.3(b); Disease Level VIII Mesothelioma may use either Expedited or Individual Review. The document then specifies disease, direct-exposure-equivalency, same-time-frame, causation, and non-Level-VIII duration/intensity requirements. *Locator: pp. 1–2.*
2. **Statute-of-limitations ADR sequence.** The ADR resolution adopts a provision that, when statute of limitations is an ADR issue, gives the Trust 30 days to send its supporting evidence; gives the claimant 30 days from receipt to respond; and requires an ADR packet after the response or expiry of that response window. *Locator: resolution p. 1; attached Amended ADR Procedures p. 2.*
3. **Procedure-document date.** The amended ADR procedures are marked **“Amended: October 30, 2025.”** This is the document’s stated amendment date; it should not be silently substituted for an individual claim deadline or a payment date. *Locator: cover page.*
