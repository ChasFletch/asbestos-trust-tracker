# DII Industries Historical-Report Recovery — 2026-09-13

## Scope and Evidence Boundary

This no-charge recovery pass examined whether publicly available materials can supply DII Industries, LLC Asbestos PI Trust annual reports for FY2005–FY2013. Those missing years are necessary before the existing FY2014–FY2025 cash-basis subtotal could be considered a complete inception-to-date series. The review did **not** use PACER purchases, paid docket services, or marketing-site payout figures.

## Verified Findings

The official DII Trust document page describes its files as publicly downloadable, but the available annual-report archive begins in FY2014. Its reported current-news links and public records do not expose the FY2005–FY2013 report sequence. The Internet Archive CDX index likewise begins with captures of the FY2014 and FY2015 annual reports; it did not return a captured FY2005–FY2013 annual-report PDF. Public court indexes identify Bankruptcy Case 03-35592, but no reviewed no-charge route supplied the missing report attachments.

| Item | Verified status | Tracker consequence |
|---|---|---|
| FY2014–FY2025 annual reports | Official archive available; existing bounded cash-basis subtotal retained | Remains a period subtotal only |
| FY2005–FY2013 annual reports | Not recovered from official archive, public index attachments, or archive captures | Do not calculate or publish inception-to-date cumulative paid |
| Bankruptcy case context | Public index confirms Case 03-35592 | Docket metadata does not replace report contents |

## Disposition

No tracker figure changed. The public Source Recovery card now states that the official and archive coverage begins in FY2014 and assigns a planned archive recheck for September 27, 2026. That date is a research-desk review date, not a prediction that a missing filing will be available.
