# Hopeman Brothers Plan Confirmed — New ~$50M Asbestos Trust
date: 2026-09-03
category: new_trust
url: https://www.mealeys.com/mealeys/articles/2515486/judge-confirms-chapter-11-plan-for-ship-subcontractor-hopeman-brothers

A Virginia bankruptcy judge **confirmed Hopeman Brothers Inc.'s Chapter 11 plan on August 18, 2026**, establishing an asbestos personal injury trust that will be funded by **nearly $50 million** from the company's insurers and the debtor's remaining assets.[1] Hopeman Brothers, a defunct "ship joiner" subcontractor that outfitted vessel interiors, filed for Chapter 11 in the Eastern District of Virginia (Case No. 24-32428) on June 30, 2024, facing roughly 126,000 historical asbestos personal injury claims, with fewer than 3,000 unresolved at filing.

The trust is not yet operational, and no opening balance or payment percentage has been published. The funding figure comes from trade-press reporting of the confirmation order, not from the filed plan document itself, and is classified accordingly.

**Source classification:** (b) secondary — trade press (Mealey's) reporting a court action; confirmation order not yet independently retrieved.
