# Owens-Illinois Trust Raises Payment Percentage to 65%
date: 2026-08-29
category: payment_percentage
url: https://www.oiasbestospersonalinjurytrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/O-I-Trust-Notice-re-Increase-Payment-Percentage.8.19.26-4905-3046-9064.1.pdf

The **Owens-Illinois Asbestos Personal Injury Trust** increased its payment percentage from **50% to 65%**, effective **August 19, 2026**. The trust’s notice states that its Trustees approved the increase after a payment-percentage review conducted with the Trust Advisory Committee and Future Claimants’ Representative.[1]

The notice also states that claimants who received payments at a lower percentage will receive a supplemental payment under §4.3 of the Trust Distribution Procedures. The new 65% percentage applies to the trust’s current payment-percentage framework; the notice does not publish a new trust balance or cumulative-payment figure.
