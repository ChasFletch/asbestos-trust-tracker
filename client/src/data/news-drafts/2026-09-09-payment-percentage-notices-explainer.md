# Payment-Percentage Notices Are Not All the Same
date: 2026-09-09
category: payment_change
url: https://www.claimsres.com/2026/09/03/manville-increase-in-the-pro-rata-payment-percentage/

Four public 2026 notices illustrate why asbestos-trust payment-percentage monitoring requires careful source reading. An implemented rate change, an interim rate pending consent, and a required reconsideration are distinct events that should not be reported as the same thing.[1]

The new analysis compares Manville’s 5.6% increase, Babcock & Wilcox’s 4.3% pending-consent rate, Federal-Mogul T&N’s 2.9% sub-account notice, and Armstrong’s reconsideration notice. Read the **full source-linked explainer** for the relevant source boundaries and direct official documents.
