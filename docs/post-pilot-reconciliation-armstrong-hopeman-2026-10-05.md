## Conclusion

Both leads are supported by controlling public material and require a correction decision. Armstrong’s October 5 notice supports a **proposed 7.8% payment percentage** that the Trust began using for interim payments under TDP §4.3 while consent from the Trust Advisory Committee (TAC) and Future Claimants’ Representative (FCR) remains pending. It should not be described simply as an unqualified, finally adopted 7.8% rate.[1]

Hopeman’s official case-agent record supports two corrections to the existing court-development article. The District Court—not the Bankruptcy Court—issued the August 18 confirmation order, and the Plan’s Effective Date occurred on September 15, 2026. The plan materials say the Asbestos Trust was established and its assets vested on the Effective Date. The reviewed documents do **not** supply a current payment percentage, asset balance, public trust portal, or a basis to offer individualized claim guidance.[2] [3]

## Armstrong World Industries Asbestos PI Trust

The Trust’s official homepage now describes the current payment percentage as **7.8%** and links a dated October 5 notice.[4] The two-page notice says the Trustees determined that the percentage **“should be decreased to 7.8%”** after the reconsideration process and supporting analysis. It then says the Trustees **requested** TAC and FCR consent to the adjustment.[1]

The notice also specifies an interim rule. Starting on the October 5 “Proposal Date,” the Trust will pay claims at the proposed 7.8% under TDP §4.3. Certain claimants with releases issued or timely transmitted before the proposal date remain paid at the former 10.8% rate, and pending court or probate approvals retain the rate in effect when the offer was first made.[1]

If 7.8% is not ultimately adopted and a higher percentage is adopted, the notice says the Trust will make supplemental payments to claims liquidated at 7.8% during the intervening period. That provision reinforces that the notice is not evidence of an unqualified final adoption.[1]

**Implemented routine transparency correction:** The tracker now displays the 7.8% figure only as a proposed interim rate, with the October 5 proposal/interim-payment date and TAC/FCR-consent qualification. It does not present the rate as a final, unconditional adoption.

## Hopeman Brothers, Inc.

The official case-agent docket identifies **Doc. 1542** as an order confirming the amended plan and approving the disclosure statement, filed on the bankruptcy docket on August 20, 2026.[2] The document’s first page identifies the underlying District Court order as **E.D. Va. Civil No. 3:26-cv-00016-DJN, Document 20, filed August 18, 2026**. Doc. 1551 expressly states that the District Court issued the confirmation order on August 18.[2] [3]

Doc. 1551, the official Notice of Entry and Occurrence of Effective Date, states that the **Effective Date occurred on September 15, 2026** and that the Plan was substantially consummated. The same notice explains that the confirmation order and plan provide a §524(g) Asbestos Permanent Channeling Injunction effective as of the Effective Date.[3]

The incorporated plan provisions identify what the effective date changes. Article VIII §8.2 deems the Asbestos Trust established, the Asbestos Trust Contribution made, and Asbestos Trust Assets vested in the trust on the Effective Date. Article VIII §8.3 says the trust is created under the plan documents, Asbestos Trust Documents, and §524(g), and will process, liquidate, and pay compensable Channeled Asbestos Claims under those documents.[2]

**Correction candidate:** Revise the Hopeman article’s stale September 3 source cut-off, court attribution, and “not yet effective” language. Preserve the absence of a verified current payment percentage, net-asset figure, cumulative payout, public claims portal, and claimant-specific instruction. This is a substantive court-status revision and should use the review pipeline before public publication.
