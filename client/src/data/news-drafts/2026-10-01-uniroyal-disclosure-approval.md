# Uniroyal Legacy Unit Wins Disclosure Statement Approval for Asbestos Trust Plan

A New Jersey federal bankruptcy judge approved the disclosure statement for the Uniroyal debtors' Chapter 11 plan on October 1, 2026. The plan contemplates establishing a § 524(g) trust to resolve current and future asbestos claims, payment in full of non-personal injury claims, and eventual dissolution of the debtor entities. The restructuring support agreement covers approximately 88% of known asbestos personal injury and wrongful death claimants.

The next steps are plan solicitation and a confirmation hearing. If confirmed, the trust would become one of the newest asbestos bankruptcy trusts, addressing 35,000 asbestos injury claims filed by the defunct tire maker's legacy unit.
