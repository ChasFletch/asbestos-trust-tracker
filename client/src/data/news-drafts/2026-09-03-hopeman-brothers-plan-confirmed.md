# Hopeman Brothers Plan Confirmed; Asbestos Trust Not Yet Open
date: 2026-09-03
category: new_trust
url: https://www.veritaglobal.net/hopeman/document/2432428260820000000000005

The official docket records an August 20, 2026 confirmation order for Hopeman Brothers Inc.'s Chapter 11 plan. The confirmed plan provides for a newly created asbestos personal-injury trust and channels specified asbestos-related liabilities to that trust under its proposed permanent channeling injunction.[1] Hopeman Brothers is a former ship-interior contractor in Chapter 11 in the Eastern District of Virginia, Case No. 24-32428.

Confirmation does **not** establish that the trust is open or accepting claims. As of September 3, 2026, the public case materials reviewed do not provide a notice that the plan is effective, a public asbestos-trust claim form or portal, an initial payment percentage, or public trust-distribution procedures. The official docket also records that an insurer appeal was opened in the Fourth Circuit after a notice of appeal was filed August 28.[2]

Trade press has reported that the proposed trust would be funded by nearly $50 million in insurer settlement proceeds and remaining assets. That amount remains **secondary reporting**, not a figure independently verified from the plan or public trust documents, and is not included in the tracker’s operating-trust figures.

**Source classification:** (a) filed confirmation order and appeal notification for the procedural status; (b) trade reporting for the reported funding description.
