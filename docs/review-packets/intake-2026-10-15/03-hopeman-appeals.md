# Hopeman appeals — bounded free-source verification

**Research date:** October 15, 2026  
**Scope:** Free CourtListener/RECAP notices and Fourth Circuit docket pages only. No PACER or paid access used. This note establishes only appellants, case numbers, originating/opening/bankruptcy-entry dates, and what the free sources do or do not reveal about the appealed district order and Liberty Mutual Fourth Circuit Doc. 38.

## Verified appeal notices and dates

The underlying bankruptcy case is **Hopeman Brothers, Inc., Bankr. E.D. Va. Case No. 24-32428**. The notices identify the originating court as the **U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Virginia at Richmond**, originating case **3:26-cv-00016-DJN**.

- **Century Indemnity Company and Westchester Fire Insurance Company — Fourth Circuit No. 26-2188.** Free docket entry **1549** is dated/filed in the bankruptcy docket **September 1, 2026**. Its notice states that the notice of appeal was filed in the originating court on **August 28, 2026**, and that the appeal **was opened** in the Fourth Circuit notice filed **August 31, 2026**. The notice names Century Indemnity Company and Westchester Fire Insurance Company as appellants. [1] The free CourtListener Fourth Circuit docket page identifies No. 26-2188 and shows docket activity dated August 31, September 1, and September 15, 2026, but the listed documents are not freely viewable there. [2]

- **St. Paul Fire and Marine Insurance Company; The Travelers Indemnity Company; and Travelers Casualty and Surety Company, f/k/a The Aetna Casualty and Surety Company — Fourth Circuit No. 26-2224.** Free RECAP/CourtListener docket entry **1550** is dated/filed in the bankruptcy docket **September 9, 2026** (the embedded appellate notice itself is marked filed September 8, 2026). It states that the notice of appeal was filed in the originating court on **September 2, 2026**, and that the appeal was opened in the Fourth Circuit notice. It names the three listed insurers as appellants. [3] The notice identifies Fourth Circuit No. 26-2224 and originating case 3:26-cv-00016-DJN. [3]

- **Liberty Mutual Insurance Company — Fourth Circuit No. 26-2191.** Free RECAP/CourtListener docket entry **1552** is dated/filed in the bankruptcy docket **September 17, 2026**. The embedded Fourth Circuit notice is marked filed **August 31, 2026**; it states that the notice of appeal was filed in the originating court on **August 28, 2026**, and that the appeal was opened. Liberty Mutual Insurance Company is the appellant. [4] The free CourtListener Fourth Circuit docket page identifies No. 26-2191, shows last-known filing activity through October 14, 2026, and lists Fourth Circuit **Document 38** dated **October 14, 2026** as an “Order filed.” The page provides only a PACER purchase link for that document, not its text. [5]

## What the free sources establish about the appealed order and Doc. 38

The appeal notices establish the **originating district case number**, but they do not state the precise district-court order being appealed. The free Justia docket for **Liberty Mutual Insurance Company v. Hopeman Brothers, Inc., Civil Action No. 3:2025cv00603**, separately shows a February 23, 2026 District Court **Filing 29**, a memorandum order adopting in part the bankruptcy judge’s recommendation, granting Hopeman’s motion to dismiss or abstain, denying other pending motions as moot, and closing that case. [6] That is a free description of a district-court order in the Liberty Mutual adversary proceeding, but the reviewed appeal notices identify **3:26-cv-00016-DJN**, not the 3:2025cv00603 docket number. On the reviewed free materials, the precise order appealed in each Fourth Circuit case therefore remains **not established**.

For **Liberty Mutual No. 26-2191**, the free Fourth Circuit page reveals only that **Doc. 38 was an order filed on October 14, 2026**. It does **not** reveal the order’s text, disposition, effect, or relationship to the district order. No merits, procedural-effect, funding, operational, claims-opening, payment, or current-trust inference is made here.

## Uncertainty / limits

The free notices and docket pages support the party names, appeal numbers, originating court and case number, notice/opening dates, and bankruptcy docket-entry dates stated above. They do not, without a freely available appellate document, establish the text or legal effect of Liberty Mutual Fourth Circuit Doc. 38. They also do not precisely identify the appealed district order beyond the originating case number. PACER and paid access were not used.
