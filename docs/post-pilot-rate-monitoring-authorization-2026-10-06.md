# Post-Pilot Payment-Rate Monitoring Authorization

**Authorization date:** October 6, 2026  
**Authorization window:** October 6–November 5, 2026, inclusive  
**Time zone:** America/Chicago  
**Status:** Active only for the limited workflow stated below; it is not a renewal of the September 6–October 5 living-tracker pilot.

## Purpose and scope

This authorization resumes **routine monitoring for asbestos-trust payment-percentage changes only**. It applies to the existing reviewed registry of official trust, administrator, case-agent, court, government, and primary-document sources. It does not authorize research or public changes outside payment-rate monitoring.

The workflow may detect and verify:

- a newly posted payment-percentage notice or current official rate statement;
- the exact prior and newly stated rate, scope (including sub-account limits), source publication date, and effective, interim, proposed, or implementation status; and
- an access failure or source replacement need that directly blocks rate verification.

The workflow may not infer a rate from a stale source, a missing document, an unchanged fingerprint, a search-result snippet, a secondary legal-marketing website, or a reader transport. A transport may identify a page for review, but the controlling official material must support any candidate.

## Cadence and resource limits

- **Weekdays at 1:00 PM Central:** review the existing source registry, candidate queue, recent run records, and coverage findings before conducting a rate-focused pass.
- **Daily cap:** at most 25 registered sources and 12 minutes for initial detection.
- **Rate-candidate review:** at most three material candidates and 25 minutes total. A single candidate may receive one extension to 60 minutes only when a controlling official source has been identified and needs reconciliation.
- **No-charge only:** no PACER purchases, paid records, paid APIs, or paid external tools.
- **No expansion by default:** new source URLs require a recorded source-registry decision before they are used for a public fact.

## Approval gates

1. **Detect and deduplicate.** Treat any fingerprint or access result as an internal signal, not a fact. Deduplicate by trust, source, and newest dated controlling event.
2. **Verify the controlling source.** Retain the source URL or identifier, document page/table where applicable, publication date, relevant effective or interim date, rate scope, and any consent or implementation qualification.
3. **Record internally.** Add the evidence, old/new values, scope, and remaining uncertainty to the existing project queue. Access-only findings stay operational records.
4. **Prepare, do not publish.** A verified rate candidate may be assembled as a proposed tracker correction or a review packet, but the workflow must not edit public data, create a public article, publish source code, or deploy.
5. **Obtain a specific release decision.** A public tracker correction requires an explicit follow-up authorization after the evidence packet is complete. A substantive article, court-development explanation, or interpretive item must use the ANCHOR → CLEO → Charles pipeline. Paul Danziger or Rod De Llano may be credited only when actual article-specific reviewer, scope, and date are recorded.

## Boundaries retained

- Keep all routine findings in the existing project queue; do not send email or Slack.
- Do not touch ads, other websites, individualized advice, new firm positions, or unrelated trust facts.
- Do not represent a proposed, interim, consent-pending, or sub-account rate as an unqualified trust-wide implemented rate.
- Missing public information remains **not publicly reported** or **unknown**, never zero.

## End condition

The schedule expires at the start of November 6, 2026, Central time. It must not be extended automatically. At expiry, stop routine checks and prepare one bounded authorization assessment summarizing rate checks, candidates, access blockers, prepared correction packets, and whether another explicit decision is warranted.
