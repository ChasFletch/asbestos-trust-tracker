# NARCO pinpoint citation — private intake note

**Verified:** October 15, 2026  
**Scope:** Source verification only. No site, database, project data, publication, deployment, or commit was changed.

## Finding

The FY2022 NARCO filing is **Case 02-20198-TPA Doc. 8512**, filed June 28, 2023. Its attached annual-report exhibit is **Exhibit A**, titled **“Annual Report, Financial Statements and Results of Operations of the North American Refractories Company Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust for Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2022.”** The audited financial statements are attached within it as **Exhibit A-1** (Exhibit A-1 PDF label, **page 1 of 23** begins the audited statements; the exhibit is identified at Exhibit A, page 10 of 10).

The exact **$280,710,185** appears in the **Special-Purpose Statements of Assets, Liabilities and Net Claimants’ Equity**, not merely in the changes statement. The statement’s printed-page label is **“4”**; the filed PDF label is **“Exhibit A-1 Page 8 of 23.”** It reports, for **December 31, 2022** (comparative December 31, 2021 amount: $465,975,373): total assets of $342,808,288, total liabilities of $62,098,103, and **net claimants’ equity of $280,710,185**. This is a year-end balance-sheet measure within the FY2022 audited special-purpose financial statements and should remain historical—not described as a current balance.

The adjacent but distinct **Special-Purpose Statements of Changes in Net Claimants’ Equity** carries printed page label **“5”** (filed PDF label **“Exhibit A-1 Page 9 of 23”**). Its 2022 column shows the change in equity and reconciles beginning equity, contributions, distributions, and ending equity; the ending-equity line also equals $280,710,185. For pinpointing the $280,710,185 balance, cite the **Statements of Assets, Liabilities and Net Claimants’ Equity (printed p. 4 / Exhibit A-1 p. 8 of 23)** and do not conflate it with the separate **Changes** statement (printed p. 5 / Exhibit A-1 p. 9 of 23).

Separately, the official **January 30, 2023 Notice of Closing on NARCO Asbestos Trust Amended Buyout Agreement** states that Honeywell **paid $1,327,153,783** to the NARCO Asbestos Trust in conjunction with closing on January 30, 2023. The same notice distinguishes that closing payment from the **$1.325 billion** lump-sum cash payment Honeywell had agreed to make under the Amended and Restated Buyout Agreement, **subject to certain adjustments**. The two amounts must not be collapsed into one figure: $1,327,153,783 is the announced closing payment; $1.325 billion is the agreed amount subject to adjustment.

## Public-surface inspection

The public NARCO trust page currently describes the buyout in its Notes as “$1.325B Honeywell buyout 1/30/2023” and displays a filed net-assets figure as of December 31, 2025. That page is not evidence that the FY2022 $280,710,185 figure is current. The public `/news` archive is reachable and presents its curated archive shell; the existing project correction-history surface documents the prior NARCO archive-card correction and its source labels. No public surface was modified during this review.

## Uncertainty / limits

The annual-report PDF is an e-service copy of the filed document and its extracted text clearly preserves both the exhibit/page labels and the printed statement labels. The $280,710,185 line is supported by the balance-sheet statement and repeated as the ending line in the Changes statement, but those are different statements with different scopes. This note makes no claim about a present NARCO balance, present directory coverage, or any amount beyond the cited historical filing and closing notice.
