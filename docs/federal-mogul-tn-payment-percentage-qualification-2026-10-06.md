## What the notice supports

The notice identifies the **T&N Sub-Account** as distinct from Federal-Mogul's other sub-accounts. It describes a proposed reduction from **3.9% to 2.9%**, subject to the consent required from the Trust Advisory Committee and Future Claimants' Representative. Under TDP §4.3, the proposed **2.9%** rate is applied while that consent process is pending.

The notice also preserves the prior **3.9%** rate for claims with all required payment documentation—including an executed release—received electronically by the stated cutoff, or transmitted by qualifying non-electronic means on or before that cutoff. The separate **FMP Sub-Account** remains distinct and is not collapsed into the T&N value.
