### Status vocabulary

- **Confirmed** — the supplied official source supports the listed official rate; qualifications in the table remain controlling.
- **Discrepancy** — the dataset needs a rate, scope, date, status, or source-conflict correction/resolution.
- **Official current rate unreported** — no current numeric rate was established in the accessible official materials. This does not mean a trust is inactive or has no payment procedure.
- **Implementation qualification** is material. “Currently applied,” “proposed,” “pending consent,” “announced pending systems work,” and “fully adopted” are not interchangeable.
