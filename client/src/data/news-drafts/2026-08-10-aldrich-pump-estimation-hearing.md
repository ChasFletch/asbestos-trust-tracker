# Aldrich Pump / Murray Boiler Asbestos Estimation Hearing Begins (Week of Aug 10, 2026)

The first phase of the asbestos-liability estimation hearing in the Chapter 11 cases of Aldrich Pump LLC and Murray Boiler LLC (Bankr. W.D.N.C. Case No. 20-30608) commenced the week of August 10, 2026, according to Trane Technologies' SEC-filed annual report. The bankruptcy court granted the Future Claims Representative's motion to streamline the estimation proceedings on December 17, 2025. The estimation result will shape the size of the §524(g) trust the Trane entities must fund; the FCR's roughly $545 million settlement framework remains unconfirmed while estimation proceeds.
