# Supreme Court Declines Bestwall Appeal; No Asbestos Trust Has Yet Formed
date: 2026-08-12
category: court_filing
url: https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-1013.html

The U.S. Supreme Court docket in *Official Committee of Asbestos Claimants of Bestwall LLC v. Bestwall LLC*, No. 25-1013, records that the petition for certiorari was **denied on June 1, 2026**. The docket shows the matter was distributed for the Court’s May 28 conference before the denial was entered.[1]

The denial ends the Supreme Court review request without creating or funding an asbestos trust. **Bestwall LLC remains in its pending Chapter 11 case in the Western District of North Carolina**, and no confirmed §524(g) trust, trust distribution procedure, payment percentage, or trust balance is currently available to add to the AsbestosTrusts.org database.

This is an important distinction for claimants and researchers: a bankruptcy case seeking a future §524(g) channeling injunction is not itself an operating asbestos trust. The tracker will continue to list Bestwall as a watch-list Chapter 11 matter rather than a trust record until a plan is confirmed and a funded trust is established.
