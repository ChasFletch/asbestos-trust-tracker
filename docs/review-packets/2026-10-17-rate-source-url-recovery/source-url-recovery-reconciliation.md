## Material reconciliation findings

### Shook & Fletcher: historical 58% notice versus 50% FAQ

- The official [May 30, 2025 payment-percentage notice](https://www.claimsres.com/wp-content/uploads/SHK-payment-notice-250602.pdf), page 1, announces an increase from 50% to 58%, effective immediately, after TAC and FCR consent. It supports a historical notice mapping for the 58% event; the PDF filename contains `250602`, but the document itself is dated May 30, 2025.
- The official [FAQ PDF](https://www.claimsres.com/wp-content/uploads/FAQs-11-19-2025_V2.pdf), page 1, Section A (General), Question 2, says: “This share, or ‘pro rata’ payment percentage, is currently 50%.” Its page header says “Last Updated May 2024.” The filename and file metadata do not, by themselves, establish a later substantive rate event.
- Neither document resolves the other. This packet does not infer a later 58%→50% reversal, call the FAQ stale, change the displayed 58%, add a source URL, change `paymentPctSourceStatus`, or claim an unqualified current rate. The single-document conflict review’s previous `MATCH_AND_MAP_SAFE` result is superseded for row 27 by this documented current-status conflict.

### Numerical figure matches but public qualification is required

- **Maremont (28.4%)** — the April 7, 2026 notice says the percentage is currently applied to *Occupationally Exposed Claims* and announces a reconsideration process; it does not establish a trust-wide final adjustment.
- **USG (10%), Combustion Engineering (15.3%), G-I Holdings (5%), Motors Liquidation (10.3%), and ACandS (2.89%)** — each recovered notice makes the figure interim, proposed, or subject to TAC/FCR consent and/or transition terms. Mapping must preserve that status rather than relabeling the number as an unqualified current rate.
- **W.R. Grace (30.1%)** — the number is also interim, with claim-offer-date transition rules. Its existing 2014 effective date conflicts with the April 2025 notice and needs a correction packet rather than a bare source URL addition.
- **NGC (45%)** — the January 19, 2023 official notice supports 40%→45%, but the separate undated official FAQ continues to say “currently 40%.” The conflict remains unresolved under `docs/ngc-payment-percentage-reconciliation-2026-10-08.md`; no public reclassification is proposed here.

### Date integrity holds

- **NARCO** — its official FAQ supports a current 100% statement but does not support the dataset’s `2013-01-01` effective date. It is last updated November 2025, without a day; that is not a substitute rate-effective date.
- **Western Asbestos Settlement Trust** — the FY2025 filed report supports 51.1% as of December 31, 2025 and identifies its 2019 history. It does not support the dataset’s `2004-01-01` effective date.
- **H.K. Porter, Christy Refractories, and United States Mineral Products** — the recovered documents establish historical rate events but do not independently restate a 2026 current figure. A public mapping must disclose the historical source date rather than label it current.
