# Armstrong World Industries Asbestos Trust Cuts Payment Percentage to 7.8%

The Armstrong World Industries Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust issued a notice dated October 5, 2026 announcing that its Trustees have determined the payment percentage should be decreased from 10.8% to 7.8% and have requested TAC and FCR consent. Under TDP §4.3, the Trust begins paying claims at the proposed 7.8% from the October 5, 2026 Proposal Date, with carve-outs for releases transmitted before that date (or within 30 days of receipt) and for deceased or incompetent claimants whose offers are pending court or probate approval. If a higher percentage is ultimately adopted, the Trust will make supplemental payments to claimants paid at 7.8% in the interim. The notice resolves the payment-percentage reconsideration the Trust announced on June 11, 2026.
