# ARTRA Primary-Source Notes — August 12, 2026

## Official trust website

The ARTRA Asbestos Trust states that it was created in 2007 following confirmation of the ARTRA Group Chapter 11 Joint Plan of Reorganization and was created to process, liquidate, and pay valid asbestos personal-injury claims under its Trust Distribution Procedures.[1]

## Official notices page

The trust’s own notices page links to a **Notice of Payment Percentage Adjustment** as well as a notice of payment-percentage adjustment and TDP amendments. The direct current-notice PDF URL exposed by the page is:

`https://www.artratrust.com/assets/uploadedFiles/6a303f42-bfb7-4d18-b4c3-7118599892e5.pdf`

That official notice states that the Trustee, with TAC and Futures Representative consent, adjusted ARTRA’s payment percentage **from 0.60% to 0.70%, effective April 1, 2025**, and that the percentage remains 0.70% until later adjusted under the TDP and Trust Agreement.[3]

## Trust Distribution Procedures

The official Second Amended TDP says the initial payment percentage was **7.5%**. It further states that, **as of September 13, 2011**, the trust, with TAC and Futures Representative consent, adjusted the payment percentage to **0.50%**. The TDP makes clear that a payment percentage can be adjusted later with TAC and Futures Representative consent, so a current rate must be taken from the later official notice rather than assumed from the TDP.[2]

## Net-assets source gap

The official ARTRA pages inspected on August 12, 2026 do not publish an annual-report archive. The Trust Agreement requires annual financial reports to the Bankruptcy Court, but no freely retrievable annual report was found during this pass. The previously proposed 2022 $18,048,181 asset figure should therefore **not** be added until a court-filed report or another traceable primary source is located.
