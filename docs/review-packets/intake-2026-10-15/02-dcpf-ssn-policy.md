# Private verification note — USG New SSN Policy and DCPF instructions

**Scope:** Bounded private research verification only. This note is not a public article, does not calculate a calendar deadline, and does not provide individual legal advice or infer any payment, queue position, claim outcome, or case result.

## Primary notice

The official USG-hosted notice is titled **“New SSN Policy”** and is dated/posted **October 12, 2026**. The same notice is also published on the Armstrong World Industries trust site. The USG page identifies the notice as posted on October 12, 2026 and states that it is posted on behalf of the nine trusts listed below. [1] [2]

The exact nine named trusts are:

1. **Armstrong World Industries, Inc. Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust**
2. **Babcock & Wilcox Company Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust**
3. **Celotex Asbestos Settlement Trust**
4. **Federal-Mogul Asbestos Personal Injury Trust**
5. **Flintkote Asbestos Trust**
6. **Owens Corning/Fibreboard Asbestos Personal Injury Trust**
7. **The Pittsburgh Corning Corporation Personal Injury Settlement Trust**
8. **United States Gypsum Asbestos Personal Injury Settlement Trust**
9. **WRG Asbestos PI Trust**

The notice’s operative filing/FIFO/statute-of-limitations language is quoted here because the distinctions matter:

> “The Trusts require the inclusion of a Social Security number (“SSN”) for a claim to be considered filed, including for purposes of the TDP statute of limitations and FIFO provisions.” [1]

The notice then states that firms have **“a 30-day grace period from the date of this notice”** to add SSNs to active or withdrawn claims missing SSNs. It says that, **“After expiration of the 30-day grace period, any claims that do not have SSNs will be rejected and any refiled claim will be subject to the Statute of Limitations and FIFO status as of the date of re-filing.”** [1]

The notice further states that it applies to **“any pending claims and claims previously submitted without an SSN that are in a Withdrawn or Deemed Withdrawn status.”** It says: **“Unless the SSN is provided within 30-days, such claims are not deemed filed for purposes of statute of limitations.”** [1]

This note preserves the notice’s stated 30-day period but does **not** convert it into a calendar deadline or calculate an individual claim date.

## Linked DCPF instructions PDF

The notice links to **“Instructions for Finding and Editing Missing SSNs,”** a three-page PDF titled **“Trust Online SSN Editing - October 2026 | DCPF.”** [3]

Page locators and contents:

- **PDF p. 1:** States that all claims filed on Trust Online must provide a valid claimant SSN and that firms are being asked to update existing claims without a valid SSN. It explains how to find missing-SSN claims using Claim Search, including filtering by Trust and **Deficiency Code 003**, defined as **“Injured Party’s Social Security Number Not Provided.”** It also describes the Deficiency Report, including its Trust, Attorney, and Deficiency Code filters and PDF/Excel/CSV export options.
- **PDF p. 2:** Explains that Trust Online allows users to edit the SSN on a **Withdrawn or Deemed Withdrawn** claim filed without an SSN. It directs the user to open the claim form from the General tab using the Claim Form button, then go to the Injured Party tab and use the pencil icon beside the SSN field.
- **PDF p. 3:** Instructs the user to click the pencil icon, enter a valid SSN, and click **Save**.

The PDF is procedural and does not add a separate trust list, payment statement, or case-result determination. Its instructions concern Trust Online searching and editing only.

## Exclusions and boundaries

The official notice names the nine trusts above. **Owens-Illinois / Paddock is not among those nine named trusts and is excluded from this notice’s stated scope.** No conclusion about an O-I/Paddock claim, payment, filing status, FIFO position, statute-of-limitations status, or case result is made here. The PDF likewise does not name O-I/Paddock and does not expand the notice’s scope.

No individual deadline is calculated. No claim-specific conclusion is drawn. The notice’s rejection and refiling language is reported as source language only, not applied to any person or claim.
